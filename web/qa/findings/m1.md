### M1-001 · major · New client dialog (date format)
- **Role:** admin
- **Repro:** Clients, New client. Look at "Financial year starts"; type 15 April 2025.
- **Expected:** date shown and typed as DD-MM-YYYY, like every other date in the app.
- **Actual:** the field is month/day/year: the default shows `04/01/2026`, and my entry showed `04/15/2025`. `04/01/2026` reads as 4 January to an Indian user, while the app means 1 April. Elsewhere the app shows 01-04-2025.
- **Evidence:** qa/screenshots/m1-newclient-badfy.png, m1-newclient-empty-err.png. The API stored fy_start 2025-04-01 for what I entered, so it is the display and entry that mislead.
- **Status:** verified (2026-09-25) - typed 15-04-2025 and 15/4/2025 (refused: must be the 1st of a month), 04-15-2025 and 31-02-2025 (refused: enter as DD-MM-YYYY), 01-04-2025 accepted; default shows 01-04-2026

## What worked well
- Client lists match the API exactly for all three roles: admin sees all, senior sees Gupta and Sharma, staff sees Sharma only; unassigned Patel shows "Not assigned" and only admin sees it. Counts read "1 client", "53 clients".
- Search is case-insensitive, ignores stray spaces, and has a helpful empty state; paging works (50 per page, "Page 1 of 2").
- New client: empty name, blank name, a non-1st date and a duplicate name (also in different case) are refused with clear messages beside the field, and nothing is created; after a good add it lands on the new client with an "added" note. Enter submits.
- Dates in lists and overview are DD-MM-YYYY.
- Theme (light/dark/device) and row density work and persist across reload; dark theme is legible. Alt+C, Ctrl+K, `?`, `g c`, Esc behave as expected.
- Console clean in normal use; the only failures were the deliberate bad inputs and refused clients.
