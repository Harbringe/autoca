### M2-001 · blocker · Upload dialog, result summary

- **Repro:** Patel & Sons, empty client. Upload `qa-patel-and-sons-2025-04.pdf` (20
  rows). Read "What happened to the rows".

## Would I sign Patel & Sons' books?

**Yes, for the mechanics — but I would refuse the sign-off in real practice, for a
reason of my own making, not a software defect.** I completed the full cycle the
coordinator asked about: sent Patel & Sons for review and signed off through
30-11-2025, and it worked cleanly — the dialog was honest about there being no
senior CA, the Overview and Clients list correctly showed what was outstanding
beforehand, and after signing off, voucher numbers ran without a single gap across
six statements and two unpost/repost cycles (Payment 1-78, Receipt 1-36, Contra
1-6), and the Trial Balance balanced exactly (₹19,91,204.00 both sides, matching
the API). But Bank Reconciliation at 30-11-2025 shows a real -₹3,01,557.96
difference, because I left June and October unuploaded on purpose. A CA cannot
sign books that don't reconcile to the bank, whatever the checklist says — and
here the checklist correctly does *not* say everything is fine: the reconciliation
report states the difference plainly. That is the software behaving exactly as it
should: it let me perform the sign-off mechanically (a real senior might sign off
a period deliberately, noting the gap, if that's the firm's practice) but it never
hid the break from me. Fill in June and October and re-run reconciliation, and I'd
sign it.

Of the original nine findings, eight are now fixed and verified: I can trust the
upload screen's own account of what it did with a statement's rows (M2-001); the
decision panel shows what I'm about to post before I post it (M2-002); "Remember
this" genuinely learns a payee and applies it to the next statement, not just the
checkbox but the whole loop (M2-003, the one I'm most glad to see actually work,
since the whole review workflow leans on it getting faster with each statement);
status wording no longer invents a senior CA that doesn't exist (M2-004); a
non-PDF upload says so in plain words (M2-005); the checklist and Clients list no
longer claim a client is "done" when a month's entries are sitting posted and
unsent (M2-006, the one I was most worried about last time); the missing-month
message no longer leaks a parameter name (M2-007); and a report's two "generated"
timestamps now agree (M2-008). M2-009 (a console 404 right after unposting an
entry) is reopened — still present on a clean, isolated re-check — though it
remains cosmetic; nothing on screen is wrong. M2-010 is new and minor: the
Overview's step 6 is still titled "Senior CA signs off" even for a client with no
senior CA, one step ahead of where the dialog underneath it has already caught up.
